## README ABOUT QUIZ GAME
Hello player! In this game we wanted to try different things for our quiz game project.
Here are the rules about it. 
You can see leaderboard or start your game.
When you start your game, you have to type your name and after that, questions are coming to your screen.
 it gives you easy questions to start slowly.
You have 15 seconds for every question.
This game has a false answer combo mechanic, so if you happen to get 3 wrong answers in a row, your game ends.
You earn more if you answered a medium or a hard question, also when you answer correctly faster, you get more points.
You have 2 different jokers. 50/50 and Time Freeze. 50/50 deletes 2 wrong answers, leaving you only 1 correct and 1 false answer (which is 50/50) and time freeze does what it says. It freezes time for you to think more.
After the game ends you can see your score and your place in scoreboard.
